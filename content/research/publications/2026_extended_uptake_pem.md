## Summary

Using clinical images acquired on the Radialis PET Imager, this modelling study assesses how image quality in low-dose [18F]FDG positron emission mammography (PEM) changes over a six-hour uptake window, by fitting kinetic models to clinical image data and quantifying the contrast-to-noise ratio over time. The results suggest delayed imaging could benefit PEM-based screening in dose-sensitive populations, such as women in routine high-risk surveillance.

Keywords: [18F]FDG PEM; breast cancer; breast imaging; delayed imaging; image quality; kinetic modelling; positron emission mammography.
